## 🚀 Featured Projects

### ⚖️ 1. Nyaya Saathi — AI Legal Accessibility Platform

**An agentic AI platform designed to make legal information more accessible.**

**Tech Stack:** Python · FastAPI · LangGraph · ChromaDB · Groq · Supabase · Twilio

- Built a seven-feature legal assistance platform covering FIR explanation, rights guidance, document drafting, bail eligibility, legal aid matching, and hearing reminders.
- Designed a seven-agent RAG architecture to ground responses in BNS, BNSS, IPC, and constitutional provisions.
- Integrated vision-based FIR extraction and structured legal-document PDF generation.
- Designed multilingual assistance supporting 10 Indian languages.
- Developed a Progressive Web App (PWA) experience.

**Links:** [📂 Source Code](https://github.com/sanketgadekar/nyaya-saathi) · [🌐 Live Demo](#)

> Legal information should be understandable and accessible. This project explores how AI can help people navigate complex legal information.

**Tech Stack:** Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · FAISS · Ollama · OpenAI

- Implemented FAISS-based vector indexing for semantic retrieval.
- Integrated language models for citation-aware responses grounded in retrieved documents.
- Built REST APIs for querying enterprise documents and maintaining persistent chat sessions.
- Designed the platform around multi-tenant document access.
